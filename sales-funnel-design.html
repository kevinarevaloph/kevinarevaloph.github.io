A practical, organic lead-generation funnel for small businesses

A clear path that turns the right audience into interested leads, builds trust, and helps move qualified prospects toward a sales conversation.

01 / Attract

Example Channels & Content

  • LinkedIn: “5 Digital Marketing Tips for Small Businesses”
  • Instagram: quick tips and behind-the-scenes posts
  • Blog: helpful guides and industry insights
  • Community: answer questions and join relevant discussions

02 / Engage

Landing Page Structure

  • Clear headline and promise
  • Problem and solution
  • Benefits and what is included
  • Testimonials or relevant examples
  • One clear call to action

03 / Capture

Example Form Fields

  • Business name
  • Contact name and email
  • Business type
  • Main marketing challenge

Keep the form short so it is easy to complete on mobile.

04 / Nurture

Example 5-Day Follow-Up

  • Day 0: confirmation and thank-you
  • Day 1: useful tip or mini-audit
  • Day 3: relevant case study or example
  • Day 5: invite them to book a call
  • Ongoing: helpful content, when appropriate

05 / Convert

What Happens in the Call

  • Understand goals and challenges
  • Recommend a suitable service
  • Answer questions and concerns
  • Send a proposal with clear next steps
↗

Tools You Can Use

  • Google Sites or a website: landing page
  • Tally or a website form: lead capture
  • Google Sheets: simple lead tracking
  • Google Analytics: traffic and conversions
  • Email platform: follow-up messages
▥

Key Metrics to Track

  • Reach: content views and website visitors
  • Engagement: clicks, replies, and time on page
  • Leads: form submissions and inquiries
  • Quality: qualified leads and calls booked
  • Revenue: clients won and sales value
✓

First 30 Days Plan

  1. Week 1Define the offer, landing page, and form.
  2. Week 2Publish useful organic content three times.
  3. Week 3Follow up with leads and share helpful examples.
  4. Week 4Review results and improve the weakest step.

Keep it simple. Be consistent. Improve with real results.
