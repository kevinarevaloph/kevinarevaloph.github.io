01 / Brand foundations

A little ritual. A better day.

Bailey is a neighborhood-minded coffee brand that brings quality and care to everyday coffee moments. Its identity balances specialty-coffee credibility with an easy, welcoming personality—considered, never intimidating.

01

Purpose

Make everyday coffee moments feel more considered, comforting, and connected.

02

Mission

Serve thoughtfully made coffee with consistent quality and genuinely warm service.

03

Vision

Become a trusted daily stop where good coffee and good energy meet.

04

Promise

A reliable cup, a welcoming experience, and details that feel intentional.

05

Personality

Warm, modern, grounded, creative, and quietly confident.

06

Positioning

Contemporary everyday coffee with specialty-minded care and neighborhood warmth.

The Bailey point of view

Thoughtful by nature. Easy by design.

Bailey should feel approachable on a busy weekday and special enough to become a small personal ritual. The brand is built on quality without fuss, design without distance, and service that feels human.

“Bailey — Your Daily Good.”

A palette with warmth and clarity.

Use teal as the signature cue, espresso to ground the identity, charcoal for legible text, cream as the main canvas, and oat as a soft supporting accent.

Usage principle: let cream and neutral space do most of the work. Use teal deliberately for recognition and interactive emphasis; reserve oat for supporting details rather than small text. Check contrast before using any color combination for body copy.

05 / Art direction

Natural texture. Honest moments.

Photography should make the coffee feel real and inviting. Prioritize tactile details, warm daylight, lived-in spaces, and people enjoying their own pace.

Coffee, up close. Crema, steam, roasted beans, pour details, and the textures of a well-made cup.
Everyday rituals. Hands, takeaway moments, quiet breaks, and genuine neighborhood energy.
Warm, not staged. Soft natural light, honest surfaces, calm compositions, and subtle grain.
01

Lighting

Prefer natural window light, gentle shadows, and warm-neutral white balance. Avoid harsh flash and heavy orange filters.

02

Composition

Leave negative space for copy, use organic shapes, and let the product remain the visual focus.

03

Graphic language

Use restrained line icons, simple shapes, soft curves, and editorial spacing. Avoid clutter and overly decorative effects.

06 / Brand in the world

One identity, many touchpoints.

Every application should feel like the same Bailey: clean, warm, considered, and easy to recognize from a distance or in a quick scroll.

Takeaway cups

Use the symbol as a bold focal point, with the wordmark clear and uncluttered. Keep print colors simple and production-friendly.

07 / Brand voice

Friendly, thoughtful, never fussy.

Bailey sounds like a knowledgeable barista who enjoys what they do and wants you to feel comfortable asking questions.

Sound like Bailey

  • Relaxed, clear, and welcoming.
  • Confident about quality without being exclusive.
  • Use simple words and specific details.
  • Make invitations feel human and low-pressure.
  • Keep copy concise, warm, and useful.

Avoid

  • Overly technical coffee language without explanation.
  • Forced slang, hype, or exaggerated promises.
  • Cold, corporate, or overly formal wording.
  • Long blocks of copy where a short line will do.
  • Claims about sourcing or sustainability unless verified.
Example line

“Your usual, made with care.”

Warm, familiar, and focused on the everyday experience.

Example line

“Take a little moment for yourself.”

Inviting without pressure or overstatement.
